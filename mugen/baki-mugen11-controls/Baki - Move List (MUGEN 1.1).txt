==========================================================================
 BAKI HANMA - BOSS VERSION - MOVE LIST (PC keyboard)
 Original character by tokage (WinMUGEN, 2014). Converted to the MUGEN 1.1
 six-button layout, and turned into a boss: smart combo AI, automatic
 meter, slow healing, hyper armor, invincible moves and an awakening.
 Easy controls: every super is one arrow + one button, and no move needs
 two buttons pressed together.
==========================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO READ THIS LIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Directions are the arrow keys, written for Baki facing RIGHT:

   Forward       = Right arrow (towards the opponent)
   Back          = Left arrow
   Up / Down     = Up arrow / Down arrow
   Down-Forward  = Down and Right pressed together
   Down-Back     = Down and Left pressed together

 When Baki faces LEFT, Forward is the Left arrow and Back the Right arrow.

 X, Y, Z, A, B, C and Start are MUGEN's buttons. To see which keyboard
 key each one is, open MUGEN's Options > Input Config.

   X = light punch    Y = medium punch    Z = heavy punch
   A = light kick     B = medium kick     C = heavy kick

 "then X" means press X straight after the arrows. Be quick: special
 moves need the whole input within 1/3 of a second.
 Tip: the in-between diagonals are optional. Down, then Forward, then
 the button also works.

 "Forward + X" means tap Forward and press X at the same time (or X right
 after the arrow). This is how every super works.

 1 bar = 1000 power. The bar now fills by itself (about 1 bar every
 5 seconds), so you don't need to hit anyone to build it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BASIC ATTACKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Standing
   X ....... Jab
   Y ....... Straight punch               (hyper armor)
   Z ....... Elbow                        (hyper armor)
   A ....... Kick
   B ....... Strong kick                  (hyper armor)
   C ....... Back kick                    (hyper armor)
   Start ... Taunt

 Crouching (hold Down)
   X ....... Crouching jab
   Y or Z .. Uppercut, launches           (hyper armor)
   A ....... Low kick
   B or C .. Sweep, knocks down           (hyper armor)

 Jumping
   X ....... Punch          Y or Z .. Strong punch
   A ....... Kick           B or C .. Strong kick

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 COMMAND ATTACKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Knee ..................... Back + A
   Middle kick .............. Forward + B                  (hyper armor)
   Jumping back kick ........ Forward, then Back, then B or C
                              (overhead: must be blocked standing)
                                                           (hyper armor)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 THROWS (stand right next to the opponent)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Throw .................... Forward + Z
   Back throw ............... Back + Z
   (Away from the opponent, Forward/Back + Z gives the elbow.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SPECIAL MOVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Himokiri (optic nerve cut)
       Down, Down-Forward, Forward, then X
       Invincible while starting. Usable until it lands once per round.

   Mach Punch
       Down, Down-Forward, Forward, then Y or Z
       Invincible until the punch comes out.

   Flying Heel Drop
       Down, Down-Forward, Forward, then A, B or C
       Invincible while starting. Unsafe if blocked.

   Whip Strike (slap)
       Down, Down-Back, Back, then X or A
       Keep holding the button to charge. While charging, hold Forward or
       Back to inch along, or press Y, Z, B or C to cancel (invincible).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SUPER MOVES (tap the arrow and press the button together)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Forward + X ........ Goutaijutsu (1 bar)
   Forward + Y ........ Triceratops Fist (2 bars)
   Forward + C ........ Cockroach Tackle (1 bar)
   Forward + A ........ Endorphin Release (1 bar, once per round)
                        Attack up for a while and a small life refill.
   Back + X ........... Trigger Counter (2 bars)
                        Unblockable against a standing opponent.
   Back + Y ........... Breathless Rush (3 bars)
   Back + B or C ...... Tiger King (1 bar)
                        Catches the opponent's attack.

 Every super except Tiger King is invincible while it starts up.
 They also work to end a combo: press them while an attack hits.

 Good to know:
   - Without enough meter, these buttons give the normal attacks.
   - The arrow has to be a fresh tap. If you are already walking
     (holding the arrow), the button gives the normal attack.
   - Down, then Forward (or Back), then the button is a special move,
     not a super.
   - The old full motions still work too, for example Down, Down-Forward,
     Forward, Down, Down-Forward, Forward, then X for Goutaijutsu.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 OTHER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Dash ..................... Forward, Forward
   Back step ................ Back, Back            (invincible start)
   Guard cancel (1 bar) ..... while blocking: Forward + Y, Z, B or C
   Air recovery ............. when knocked into the air: any attack button
   Wall jump ................ jump next to the screen edge, then hold
                              Up + the arrow pointing away from the wall

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BOSS FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Automatic meter: the bar fills by itself, about 1 bar every 5 seconds.

 Slow healing: Baki gets back 3 life per second (full life is 1000)
 while he isn't being hit.

 Hyper armor: the attacks marked "(hyper armor)" absorb one hit. Baki
 flashes red, takes the damage and keeps attacking. Throws beat armor.

 AWAKENING: the first time his life drops to 35% in a round, Baki
 awakens (flash, sound, red after-image trail). Until the round ends:
   - his meter jumps to full and stays full
   - his normal attacks use faster animations
   - constant hyper armor: hits don't knock him back while he attacks,
     moves or stands (he still takes the damage; throws still work)
   - the AI goes all-out

 Boss AI (when the computer controls Baki):
   - Gets more aggressive the lower his life is: blocks 95% of attacks
     at full life, but at low life he swings through your attacks with
     armored punches instead.
   - Picks one of 8 combo routes every time he attacks, so his combos
     change, and every link is timed to connect:
       1. crouch jab, straight, strong kick, then Breathless Rush,
          Goutaijutsu or Mach Punch
       2. jab, elbow, back kick, then Trigger Counter, Cockroach Tackle
          or Mach Punch
       3. low kick, middle kick, then Triceratops Fist, Himokiri or
          Whip Strike
       4. crouch jab, straight, uppercut (launch) into an air combo, or
          a sweep
       5. low kick, straight, sweep
       6. kick, knee, elbow, middle kick, then Goutaijutsu or Whip Strike
       7. jab, straight, then Cockroach Tackle or Mach Punch
       8. crouch jab, straight, back kick, then Breathless Rush, Heel Drop
          (in the corner) or Whip Strike
     He uses supers to end combos whenever he has the meter.
   - Counters when he can't block: if you attack while he is stuck in
     his own attack, he cancels it into an invincible move; he escapes
     throws with the Heel Drop.
   - Reversals: Breathless Rush, Trigger Counter, Tiger King, Heel Drop
     and Mach Punch against attacks started close to him.
   - Moves around: runs and walks in, jumps in, backs off, and closes in
     on you when you're knocked down.
   - Uses his specials in neutral (Mach Punch pokes, Heel Drop, Whip
     Strike, Himokiri), overheads against crouching guards, throws,
     anti-airs, guard cancels and long-range supers.
   - Takes 20% less damage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 TUNING THE BOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Open baki-2.cns in Notepad. The BOSS SETTINGS are at the top:
   var(56) = 1 ...... who gets the boss powers (meter, healing, armor,
                      invincibility, awakening): 1 = Baki always,
                      2 = only the computer's Baki, 0 = nobody
   var(57) = 1 ...... boss AI (0 = the original, easier AI)
   value = 10 ....... (under "automatic meter") power every 3 frames:
                      bigger = faster, 0 = off
   % 20 ............. (under "passive healing") heal once every 20
                      frames: bigger = slower
   value = 1.25 ..... (under "CPU toughness") 1 = normal damage
   * 35 / 100 ....... (under "Awakening: start") awaken at 35% life
 Save the file in the same encoding it opened with (ANSI). Don't save it
 as UTF-8.

 Even harder: palettes 11 and 12 are the original author's bonus colors
 (more damage, faster attacks). Palette 12 also revives once when KO'd.
 To make the computer use palette 12 when it picks Baki, open baki.def
 and change "pal.defaults = 1,2,3" to "pal.defaults = 12".

 The AI switches on as soon as MUGEN's computer player starts pressing
 buttons. If Baki stands still for a moment at the start of a round,
 raise the difficulty in MUGEN's Options.
