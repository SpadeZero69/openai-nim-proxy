==========================================================================
 BROLY SSJ5 ("Limit-Breaking Broly") - MOVE LIST (PC keyboard) - MUGEN 1.1
 Original character by the author of the kamasetoyobanaide blog, based on
 gulthor's The Nightmare SSJ3HD. Package author in the .def: dgx.
 Made loadable in MUGEN 1.1; the moves and the AI are unchanged.
==========================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO READ THIS LIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Directions are the arrow keys, written for Broly facing RIGHT:

   Forward       = Right arrow (towards the opponent)
   Back          = Left arrow
   Up / Down     = Up arrow / Down arrow
   Down-Forward  = Down and Right pressed together

 When Broly faces LEFT, Forward is the Left arrow and Back the Right arrow.

 A, B, C, X, Y, Z and Start are MUGEN's buttons. To see which keyboard
 key each one is, open MUGEN's Options > Input Config.

   X / Y / Z = light / medium / strong punch
   A / B / C = light / medium / strong kick
   Start     = taunt

 "Forward + X" means hold Forward and press X.
 "X + Y" means press X and Y at the same time.
 "Back, Forward, A" means tap Back, tap Forward, then press A, quickly
 (all within 1/4 of a second).

 Power: 1 bar = 1000. It fills by itself during the round (about a bar
 every 12 seconds in palettes 1-2, faster in the others), and Power Up
 fills it faster. In this download his gauge holds 2.5 bars (2500); see
 "Big supers" for what that means.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BASIC ATTACKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Standing
   X, Y, Z ..... light, medium, strong punch
   A, B, C ..... light, medium, strong kick
   Start ....... taunt (not in SSJ5 mode)

 Crouching (hold Down)
   X, Y or Z ... crouching punch
   A, B or C ... crouching kick

 Jumping
   any button .. jump kick

 Chains (press the next button while the previous hit connects)
   X, X, Y ..... light, medium, strong punch
   A, A, B ..... light, medium, strong kick
   On hit, a light or medium attack can also go into Y, Z, B or C, and
   most hits can be cancelled into the moves below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 DIRECTION + BUTTON MOVES (hold the arrow, press the button)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Forward + X .... Atomic Bomber: a pillar of energy
   Forward + A .... Smasher Lariat: rushes at the opponent
   Back + A ....... Smasher Lariat EX (needs 1/2 bar)
   Forward + B .... Machine Gun Shooter: kicks the opponent up fast
   Back + B ....... Machine Gun Shooter EX (needs 1/2 bar)
   Back + X ....... Energy Barrage: a stream of big energy blasts
   Forward + C .... Ki Blast: a fast energy ball (uses a little power)
   Back + C ....... Uppercut
   Back + Y ....... Dynamite Kick
   Forward + Y .... Throw
   Forward + Z .... Throw (another throw)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 TWO-BUTTON MOVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Hold A + X ..... Power Up: charges ki, gives back power and life
   Hold B + Y ..... Shockwave: uses some power to blow the opponent away
   X + Y .......... Flight (on the ground or in the air). While flying
                    the attack buttons still work, and A + B is the
                    Fall Down Kick.
   A + B (air) .... Fall Down Kick: dives down with a kick. While flying,
                    or after a jump kick that connects.
   Y + Z .......... Teleport. Also works while you are being hit, to get
                    away. When awakened he fires a few ki blasts first.
   Y + Z .......... Emergency Dodge (LSSJ and SSJ5 modes, while
                    attacking): appears behind the opponent. You can
                    keep attacking, but not with special moves.
   Z + C .......... Awaken (see "Awakening and modes")

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SUPER MOVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Back, Forward, A ...... K.F.M.S.W.A: a rush of blows (1 bar)
   Back, Forward, B ...... Eraser Cannon (1 bar)
   Back, Forward, C ...... Blast Buster: grabs and carries the opponent
                           (1 bar)
   Back, Forward, X ...... Meteor Smash: a rush that ends by slamming the
                           opponent into the ground (1 bar)
   Back, Forward, Y ...... Kamehameha (1 1/2 bars)
   Back, Forward, Z ...... Ultimate Rush: dashes in, then one of three
                           moves at random (2 bars; not in SSJ5 mode)
   Down, Forward, Back, Z  Energy Barrage EX

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BIG SUPERS (Down, Down-Forward, Forward, then the button)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ... A ... Super Spirit Bomb .......... needs 7 bars
   ... B ... Dark Force ................. needs 4 bars
   ... C ... Supernova (as SSJ5) ........ needs more than 9 bars
   ... X ... Voltenic Ultima ............ needs 6 bars
   ... Y ... Earth Breaker .............. needs 6 bars (ground only)
   ... Z ... Enemy Burst ................ needs 5 bars

 Important: in this download his gauge holds only 2.5 bars, so in
 palettes 1-11 you can't do these (the original behaves the same way).
 Palette 12 skips the power check for all of them except Earth Breaker.
 When the computer plays him in palettes 5-11 it also counts a hidden
 power meter that goes up to 10 bars, so the computer can use them.
 To unlock them, open broly_data.cns and change the line  power=2500
 to  power=10000  (the gauge then holds 10 bars, and the computer
 uses these moves too).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 AWAKENING AND MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Z + C (palettes 3-12, full gauge, at least 501 life):
   Legendary Super Saiyan (LSSJ) mode.
 Z + C again in LSSJ mode (needs 10 bars, so with the 2.5-bar gauge only
 palette 12 can do it; the computer can in palettes 5-11): SSJ5 mode.
 To start a match in SSJ5 mode, see "Settings".
 Palettes 1 and 2 can't awaken.

 LSSJ mode: no juggle limit, Emergency Dodge, most normal attacks break
 armor, power fills more slowly.
 SSJ5 mode: power gauge always full, much tougher, every attack is
 unblockable, some moves change, he slowly heals below 40% life. When
 the time runs out (about 40 seconds) his life drops to 1, he turns back
 and can't move for a while.

 Extra moves in SSJ5 mode:
   Back, Forward, B ...... Gigantic Meteor (after Eraser Cannon)
   Back + X .............. Scatter Blasts: U-shaped energy blasts
   Forward + C ........... Ki Wave: a faster beam
   Down, Forward, Back, Z  Three-Way Barrage
   Hold B + Y ............ Super Shockwave
   Z + C ................. Ultima Field: lightning falls for 700 frames
                           (stacks; permanent with the Counter Barrier)
   Back, Forward, Z + C .. Counter Barrier (palettes 7-12, after losing a
                           round): reflects attacks, powers up some moves

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PALETTES (each one plays differently)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 On the character select screen, A, B, C, X, Y, Z pick palettes 1-6.
 Hold Start and press them for palettes 7-12.

   1, 2   Normal. Strong but fair; can't awaken.
   3, 4   Very strong: permanent armor, invincible Power Up, can awaken.
   5, 6   Stronger again: life protection, armor, faster gauge when low
          on life, starts round 2 with a full gauge.
   7, 8   "God" level: much more damage, instant-kill tricks, heals when
          low on life. SSJ5 lasts twice as long.
   9, 10  Tougher god level. SSJ5 lasts three times as long.
   11     Can't be damaged normally.
   12     Strongest: uses every trick, starts with a full gauge.
          It has the same colors as palette 1, so check which one you
          picked.
 Palettes 7-12 are made to fight other "god" characters; they can end a
 match against normal characters almost instantly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SETTINGS (top of broly_command.cns, open it with Notepad)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   trigger1=var(4):=5     AI level, 1-5 (5 = strongest). 0 turns his AI
                          off. If he ever moves on his own while you
                          play him, set this to 0.
   trigger1=var(10):=0    Starting mode for palettes 3-11:
                          0 = normal, 1 = LSSJ, 2 = SSJ5.
   trigger1=var(35):=2400 SSJ5 time in ticks (2400 = about 40 seconds).
                          -1 = SSJ5 never ends.
 Change only the number before the ";".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MUGEN 1.1 NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Install: copy the whole "Broly SSJ5" folder into MUGEN's chars folder,
 then add a line with  Broly SSJ5  under [Characters] in data/select.def.

 He stays a WinMUGEN-style character (mugenversion is empty in the
 .def), so MUGEN 1.1 runs him with the old engine rules his tricks were
 built on. The conversion only changes lines MUGEN 1.1 can refuse to
 load, and keeps what they did; the changed lines are marked with
 ";MUGEN 1.1 conversion" in the files. One thing can't work in 1.1:
 MUGEN 1.1 has no TagIn controller, so a trick that changed the state of
 the opponent's partner in team matches is switched off.
