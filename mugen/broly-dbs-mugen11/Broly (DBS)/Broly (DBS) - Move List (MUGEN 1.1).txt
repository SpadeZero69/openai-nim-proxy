==========================================================================
 BROLY (DBS) - MOVE LIST (PC keyboard) - MUGEN 1.1
 Character by Blue (sprites by Blue and Kimex), "infinite scaling" edit
 by coldskin1. Converted to MUGEN 1.1.
==========================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO READ THIS LIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Directions are the arrow keys, written for Broly facing RIGHT:

   Forward       = Right arrow (towards the opponent)
   Back          = Left arrow
   Up / Down     = Up arrow / Down arrow
   Down-Forward  = Down and Right pressed together
   Down-Back     = Down and Left pressed together

 When Broly faces LEFT, Forward is the Left arrow and Back the Right arrow.

 A, B, C, X, Y, Z and Start are MUGEN's buttons. To see which keyboard
 key each one is, open MUGEN's Options > Input Config.

 Broly uses every button:

   A = punch string       B = kick string        C = ki blast
   X = transform          Y = dash strike        Z = ki burst
   Start (hold) = charge ki

 "then A" means press A straight after the arrows. Be quick:
   special moves: within 1/3 of a second
   ultimate:      within 1/2 of a second
 Tip: the in-between diagonals are optional. Down, then Forward, then
 the button also works.

 Power: 4 bars (1 bar = 1000). Hold Start to charge it. Some moves only
 need a certain amount of power and don't use it up; the list says so.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BASIC ATTACKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Standing
   A ....... Punch
   B ....... Kick
   C ....... Ki blast (needs a little power). Hold C to keep firing, up
             to 5 blasts.
   Start ... Hold to charge ki (stand still; stops when the bar is full)

 Crouching (hold Down, then press the button)
   A ....... Ki explosion around Broly. Invincible. (needs 1/2 bar, not
             used up)
   B ....... Flip kick (also the last hit of the kick string)
   C ....... Energy sphere: a ki aura around Broly that hits several
             times (needs 1/2 bar, not used up)

 Jumping
   A ....... Punch
   B ....... Kick
   C ....... Air grab. When it connects, press C again to slam them.
   Down + A  Ki explosion. Invincible. (needs 1/2 bar, not used up)
   Down + B  Dive stomp, only when high in the air (needs 1/4 bar, not
             used up)
   Down + C  Ki ball thrown down at an angle. Can't be blocked.
             (needs 3/4 bar, not used up)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BUTTON MOVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Dash strike ........ Y, on the ground or in the air
       (needs 1/2 bar, not used up)
       A very fast flying strike at the opponent.

   Ki burst ........... Z
       An energy wave in front of Broly. It can't be blocked, knocks the
       opponent back and stuns them. Works while you are blocking or
       being hit, so use it to escape. Recharges in about 8 seconds (a
       flash shows when it's ready). Not while attacking, and not when
       the opponent is cornered right in front of you.

   Transform .......... X, standing still (2 bars)
       Legendary Super Saiyan: heals 500 life, faster dashes, much
       stronger armor (see "The longer the fight" below).
       Press X again to power down (gives back 1 bar).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 CHAINS (press the next button while the previous hit connects)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Punch string ....... A, A, A
       The third A is a flying tackle that knocks the opponent into the
       air, and Broly goes up with them. Continue in the air with:
         A, A or B, then Down + B (dive stomp)
         C (air grab), then C again (slam)
         Down + A (ki explosion)

   Kick string ........ B, B, B, B, B, B, then Y
       Six kicks, the last one is the flip kick, finished with the dash
       strike (needs 1/2 bar). You can switch to the punch string with A
       after the 1st, 3rd, 4th or 5th kick, and from the punch string to
       the kick string with B after the first A.

   After a jumping A or B you can cancel straight into the dive stomp,
   the air ki ball or the ki barrage.

   Y (dash strike) also follows up after: the flip kick, the dive stomp,
   the charged punch and the energy grab.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SPECIAL MOVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Charged punch
       Down, Down-Forward, Forward, then A        (needs 1/2 bar, not used)
       Keep holding A to charge (invincible while charging), let go to
       dash in with a punch. Hold it for more than about 1 second for the
       strong version, which can't be blocked.

   Rushing assault (1 bar)
       Down, Down-Back, Back, then A
       Invincible charge. Can't be blocked. On hit, a rush combo.

   Grab and smash (1 bar)
       Down, Down-Forward, Forward, then B
       Lunging grab, then he swings the opponent around and slams them.

   Grab and drag (1 bar)
       Down, Down-Back, Back, then B
       Lunging grab, then he drags the opponent along and smashes them.

   Ki barrage (1 bar, needs 1 1/2 bars to start)
       Down, Down-Forward, Forward, then C        (ground or air)
       Invincible. He rises and fires a long barrage of ki blasts, then a
       rain of energy falls from the sky. Can't be blocked.

   Energy grab (1 bar)
       Down, Down-Back, Back, then C
       Invincible lunging grab that ends with a ki blast. Y follows up.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ULTIMATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Ultimate (2 bars, needs 2 1/2 bars to start)
       Down, Down-Forward, Forward, Down, Down-Forward, Forward,
       then A and B together
       Invincible charge that can't be blocked. On hit, a cinematic
       finisher. Only in 1-on-1 matches (not in team modes).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MOVEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Run / fly forward ...... Forward, Forward (hold Forward to keep going)
   Back dash .............. Back, Back (hold Back to keep going)
   Super jump ............. Down, then Up (quickly)
   Air dash ............... in the air: Forward, Forward or Back, Back
   Recover in the air ..... when knocked into the air, press two buttons
                            together (A+B, B+C, A+C, X+Y, Y+Z or X+Z)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 THE LONGER THE FIGHT, THE STRONGER HE GETS (no limit)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Broly has a power level that goes up by 1 every second of fighting,
 starting at 1, so it is 61 after one minute. It never stops and it
 carries over from round to round. With every level:
   - Damage: his attacks hit harder (x level).
   - Durability: he takes less damage (damage divided by the level), and
     his armor absorbs one more hit before he flinches.
   - Regeneration: he heals faster, by 0.2% of his max life per second
     for each level. He heals all the time during a round.
 While he is transformed, damage, durability and armor are 8 times
 higher still (regeneration stays the same).

   Fighting time  Level   Damage   Damage taken   Armor hits   Healing
   start             1      x1      full             1        0.2% a second
   30 seconds       31      x31     1/31            31        6.2% a second
   1 minute         61      x61     1/61            61       12.2% a second
   2 minutes       121      x121    1/121          121       24.2% a second
   5 minutes       301      x301    1/301          301       60.2% a second

 His attacks also take away a share of the opponent's maximum life, so
 they hurt every opponent the same. When the computer controls him, he
 doesn't block while the armor is up.
 Original "infinite scaling" edit by coldskin1; the regeneration and the
 faster level were added in this version.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 AFTER ONE MINUTE: NOTHING STOPS HIS ATTACKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 From one minute of fighting on, for the rest of the match, every attack
 of his (and of his ki blasts and other helpers):
   - reaches the whole screen: it hits the opponent wherever they are,
     in front of him or behind him;
   - can't be blocked;
   - goes through invincibility: invincible moves, dodges, rolls,
     wake-up invincibility, parries and counters don't stop it;
   - hits opponents in the air, falling or lying on the ground.
 When the computer controls him, he attacks from any distance.

 Two things still beat it: MUGEN can't hit an opponent whose current
 animation frame has no hurt box at all, and a few characters only let
 chosen attacks hit them (HitBy), which his attacks don't match.
 Fighting time counts only while the round is on (not intros), pauses
 included.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MUGEN 1.1 NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Install: copy the whole "Broly (DBS)" folder (with the "-" folder inside)
 into MUGEN's chars folder, then add a line with  Broly (DBS)  under
 [Characters] in data/select.def.

 This version is declared as a MUGEN 1.1 character. Special moves accept
 a slower input (1/3 second instead of 1/4).
